# ATC ↔ KI-Pilot Sprechfunk (experimental)
On main radar page: login, join room, claim matching position. Tap `Voice` to enable pilot audio, then `Speak`, allow microphone, and say e.g. `DLH772 taxi`, `EWG204 cleared to land`, `DLH772 heading 270`, `DLH772 climb 5000`.
Browser SpeechRecognition converts speech to text; client maps recognized English phrases to existing server-authorized commands. Server validates sector and aircraft, then replies. Browser speechSynthesis reads back a pilot acknowledgement. Readback is simplified and may not exactly reflect aircraft behavior; commands unsupported by the existing engine are not implemented.
Requires browser speech-recognition support and microphone permission; on iOS availability varies. No microphone audio is uploaded to our own server, but browser speech-recognition providers may process audio remotely. No real player-to-player voice, no continuous radio stream, no realistic frequency management. For safety this is only a game, not a real ATC training tool.
